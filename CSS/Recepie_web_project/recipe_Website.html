Gulab Jamun


Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Bread
  • Oil

Recepie

  1. Mix khoya , all-purpose flour, and a pinch of baking soda to form a soft dough.
  2. Roll small, smooth balls from the dough and deep-fry them in ghee or oil over low heat .
  3. Prepare a sugar syrup by boiling sugar, water, and cardamom pods.
  4. Transfer the fried gulab jamuns into the warm sugar syrup and let them soak .
